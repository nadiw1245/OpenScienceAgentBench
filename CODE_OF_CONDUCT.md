# Code of Conduct

OpenScienceAgentBench is a community project for people exploring research and reproducible science. Participants are expected to be respectful, constructive, and considerate of differing experience levels and backgrounds.

Examples of welcome behavior include asking clear questions, citing sources, explaining disagreements, and giving actionable feedback. Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, deliberate disruption, and publishing another person's private information are not acceptable.

Project maintainers may edit or remove contributions that violate these expectations and may restrict participation when needed to keep the community safe. For conduct concerns, contact the repository owner through their GitHub profile. Do not post sensitive personal information in a public issue.
